# Poland-to-Netherlands flooring price snapshot

**Reviewed:** 25 October 2026  
**Scope:** One Polish SPC click-vinyl product and a Netherlands published installation-cost guide. This is a partial planning subtotal, not a supplier quote or a complete delivered renovation price.

## Source facts

### Polish material listing

- **Publisher/seller:** Castorama Polska, [Panel winylowy SPC Dąb Orzechowy, 2.24 m²](https://www.castorama.pl/panel-winylowy-spc-dab-orzechowy-2-24-m2/5063022943248_CAPL.prd).
- **Price observed:** PLN 156.76 per 2.24 m² pack, or approximately PLN 69.98/m² by division. The listing is a point-in-time consumer price, observed 25 October 2026; it is not a price feed.
- **Listed product details:** 8 planks/package; use class 32; 0.4 mm wear layer; CE marking; A+ VOC emissions class; stated 20-year manufacturer warranty. The page showed no customer rating when reviewed. These are listing/manufacturer claims, not an independent product-quality assessment.
- **Installation constraints stated by the listing:** A separate 1 mm underlay is required. The product is not intended for a shower zone or areas exposed to prolonged direct sunlight. Confirm suitability for the actual room and the installer’s requirements.
- **VAT and cross-border delivery:** The product page’s VAT basis was not clearly verified. Delivery to the Netherlands, cross-border sales eligibility, Dutch warranty/returns handling, and transport cost were not established.

### Netherlands installation labour guide

- **Publisher:** Click-PVC, [Wat kost een click PVC vloer? Prijs per m² in 2026](https://click-pvc.com/blogs/kennisbank-click-pvc-vloeren/click-pvc-vloer-prijzen-2026).
- **Reported labour:** €35–€75/m² for professional PVC floor installation, explicitly excluding the floor itself. The page distinguishes €35–€50/m² for straightforward conditions and €50–€75/m² where extra preparation or patterns apply.
- **Method and limitations:** The publisher is a commercial flooring retailer. The article does not identify a sample, local installer quote, labour-only invoice dataset, regional adjustment, or VAT treatment. These are guide prices, not an offer from a selected or quality-verified installer.
- **Material comparison on that page:** It states €19.95–€45/m² for quality click-PVC material in 2026. The Castorama listing's package price converts to about €15.92/m² at the ECB rate used here. This is not a like-for-like comparison with the Castorama SKU; no savings or equivalent quality claim is inferred.

### Currency conversion

- **Publisher:** European Central Bank, [Euro reference exchange rate: Polish zloty](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-pln.en.html).
- **Rate used:** On 23 October 2026, €1 = PLN 4.3945 (the latest published business-day rate available on the Sunday review date).
- The ECB rate is a reference conversion only; it excludes card/bank exchange spreads and payment fees.

## Explicit exclusions and interpretation

- This uses one Polish product example; the app does not claim it is the best-value product or that it is technically equivalent to Dutch products in the Click-PVC guide.
- Polish-to-Netherlands transport, delivery insurance, product availability, VAT basis, bank fees, the required 1 mm underlay, floor removal, levelling/preparation, trims, and other finishing materials are not priced. Therefore the displayed subtotal is incomplete and may not be purchasable at that total.
- The installation range is not a quote, and neither the retailer nor an installer has been selected or independently quality-rated for this project.
- No labour/material split was derived from a combined total. Material and labour figures come from separate, explicitly priced source statements.
- The app stores the reviewed prices as static snapshots. It does not automatically refresh product prices, exchange rates, or labour guides; recheck the linked sources before purchasing or booking.
