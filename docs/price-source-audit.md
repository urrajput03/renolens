# Public price-source audit: Dutch home renovation

## Scope and evidence standard

This audit checks public price-guide pages for bathroom, toilet-room, kitchen, flooring, and interior-painting work in the Netherlands. “Verified” below means that the statement was present on the cited page when reviewed; it does not mean that the price was independently verified against completed jobs or contractor invoices.

All five category pages reviewed are published by Homedeal. They are consumer price guides on a commercial contractor-comparison/quote-request site, not a representative transaction database. No independent second price source was verified for these ranges in this audit. Treat the figures as attributed guide prices, not as validated national market estimates.

## Category findings

### Bathroom

- **Source/publisher:** Homedeal, [Badkamer renovatie kosten](https://www.homedeal.nl/badkamerspecialist/badkamer-renoveren-kosten/).
- **Publication/update:** First published 28 July 2022; last modified 23 April 2026 (page metadata).
- **Reported prices and units:** €3,500–€15,000 for a complete bathroom of about 9 m²; separately, €500–€1,200 per m².
- **Included work:** The page describes the complete-room figure as including labour, materials, and installation. Its price tables also label component prices as including installation, materials, and VAT; the page does not make the basis of every displayed range equally clear.
- **VAT:** VAT is indicated in table pricing, but do not assume that every headline and per-m² figure has an identical tax basis without confirming it with Homedeal.
- **Regional coverage:** The page says prices in the Randstad are often higher. It does not provide a quantified regional series or adjustment factor.
- **Stated methodology:** Prices are presented through room/finish scenarios and cost factors. No sample size, contractor-invoice dataset, survey method, or calculation formula is disclosed.
- **Comparability caveat:** The two headline measures do not reconcile cleanly: 9 m² at €500–€1,200/m² implies €4,500–€10,800, not €3,500–€15,000. Their scopes may differ; do not combine or normalize them without clarification.

### Toilet room

- **Source/publisher:** Homedeal, [Toilet verbouwen kosten](https://www.homedeal.nl/verbouwing/toilet-verbouwen-kosten/).
- **Publication/update:** First published 10 January 2024; modified/displayed 8 July 2026 (page metadata).
- **Reported prices and units:** €1,300–€4,500 for a toilet-room renovation. Its table lists a basic scope at €1,200–€2,000 and an average scope at €2,500–€4,000. A separate luxury example for a 2 m² room is €4,000–€6,000, with higher-end work stated as reaching €8,000 or more. Replacing only the toilet is separately priced at €300–€700 including installation.
- **Included work:** The main renovation range says it includes demolition, materials, and installation. The table describes example fixture/tile scopes.
- **VAT:** Not stated clearly for the main range in the reviewed page text.
- **Regional coverage:** No quantified regional coverage or adjustment is stated.
- **Stated methodology:** Scenario descriptions based on renovation scope and finish; no sample, quote dataset, or calculation formula is disclosed.
- **Comparability caveat:** The €4,500 headline upper end is not a maximum for all scopes: the same page’s luxury example goes higher. Keep the headline range and luxury example distinct.

### Kitchen

- **Source/publisher:** Homedeal, [Keukenrenovatie kosten](https://www.homedeal.nl/keukenspecialist/keukenrenovatie-kosten/).
- **Publication/update:** First published 6 November 2015; last modified 26 November 2025 (page metadata).
- **Reported prices and units:** Package prices by the number of fronts/panels: small kitchen €350–€950 (3 doors, 3 drawer fronts, refrigerator panel); average €900–€2,000 (8 doors, 5 drawer fronts, refrigerator and dishwasher panels); large €1,000–€3,000 (12 doors, 7 drawer fronts, refrigerator and dishwasher panels). The page also gives an average of about €1,400 including installation.
- **Included work:** The table says prices include VAT and placement. These examples principally describe a refresh/replacement of cabinet fronts and panels, not a full kitchen replacement with all demolition, cabinets, appliances, plumbing, and electrical work.
- **VAT:** Explicitly included in the table’s price heading.
- **Regional coverage:** No quantified regional coverage or adjustment is stated.
- **Stated methodology:** Examples are grouped by kitchen/front count. No sample, quote dataset, or calculation formula is disclosed. A graphic states a 70% materials / 30% labour composition, but gives no supporting derivation or method; this audit does not treat it as a transferable cost split.
- **Comparability caveat:** Do not use this page as a complete-kitchen renovation benchmark unless the user’s scope matches the front/panel refresh examples.

### Flooring

- **Source/publisher:** Homedeal, [Vloer laten leggen](https://www.homedeal.nl/vloeren/vloer-leggen/).
- **Publication/update:** Page display date 12 February 2026; page metadata shows publication on 13 February 2026. No later update date was established.
- **Reported prices and units:** Per m², “including laying”: laminate €20–€40; PVC/vinyl €30–€60; wood/parket €50–€120; tile/natural stone €45–€90; cast floor €60–€150. A 50 m² laminate example is €1,000–€2,000 including material and placement. Laminate laying alone is separately stated as €15–€25/m²; levelling is an additional €10–€25/m².
- **Included work:** The material-specific table includes laying; it does not establish that every preparation or finishing item (such as underlay, skirting, removal of the old floor, or levelling) is included. The article lists some of these as additional costs.
- **VAT:** Not stated clearly for the reviewed per-m² table.
- **Regional coverage:** No quantified regional coverage or adjustment is stated.
- **Stated methodology:** Rates are grouped by flooring type, with a 50 m² laminate example and separate add-ons. No sample, quote dataset, or calculation formula is disclosed.
- **Comparability caveat:** The page’s general summary says €20–€120/m², while its own table lists cast floors up to €150/m². Preserve the material-specific values and flag the mismatch. The labour-only figure is specific to laminate; do not generalize it to other floor types.

### Interior painting

- **Source/publisher:** Homedeal, [Schilder kosten](https://www.homedeal.nl/schilderwerk/schilder-kosten/).
- **Publication/update:** First published 28 September 2023; last modified 10 October 2025 (page metadata).
- **Reported prices and units:** Interior painting €30–€35/m²; the page also reports €35–€40 per hour for a painter. These are distinct pricing units and should not be converted into each other without a supported productivity assumption.
- **Included work:** The page labels its price table as including paint, materials, and VAT.
- **VAT:** Homedeal states that a reduced 9% VAT rate applies to painting costs when a home is more than two years old. This is a claim on the commercial guide, not an official tax determination; current Belastingdienst guidance and the specific work/eligibility should be checked before applying it.
- **Regional coverage:** The page says remote locations or hard-to-reach areas may cost more and that Randstad/large-city prices may be higher because of demand. It gives no quantified regional adjustments.
- **Stated methodology:** Average prices are presented by project type and unit. No sample, quote dataset, or calculation formula is disclosed.

## Cross-cutting limitations and use

### Verified facts vs. assumptions

- **Verified from the cited pages:** The displayed ranges, units, scope descriptions, page dates, and VAT statements recorded above.
- **Not verified:** Whether the guides are based on representative completed jobs, current contractor quotes, a defined sample, or a reproducible method; whether the figures are national averages; and whether separate categories use consistent definitions of “including installation,” materials, or VAT.
- **Assumption to avoid:** A consumer-facing Dutch site does not by itself establish nationally representative coverage. Do not label these values “average Netherlands prices” unless the publisher’s sampling and geographic coverage support that claim.
- **Do not infer cost splits:** Except for noting Homedeal’s kitchen graphic as an unsupported publisher claim, this audit does not derive labour/material shares from combined prices. Do not use aggregate totals to fabricate the three contracting scenarios.

### Commercial interest and licensing

Homedeal’s pages invite users to compare professionals and request free quotes, so the publisher has a commercial interest in connecting renovation customers with contractors. Its [legal/disclaimer page](https://www.homedeal.nl/legal/) says it cannot guarantee completeness, correctness, or continuing currency of site information and recommends users conduct further checks.

No open-data licence or explicit permission to republish these price guides, article text, or images was established in this audit. Public accessibility is not a reuse licence. Obtain written permission or a suitable licence before bulk reproducing the content/rates; link to and attribute the pages for research references. Official VAT guidance was not independently retrieved during this audit, so the painting tax statement remains unverified here.

### Recommendation for the estimator

These five Homedeal guides are not sufficient to support source-backed, itemized estimates for every category. The current flooring pilot instead uses a dated individual Polish product listing and a Netherlands installation-cost guide; its specific sources and limitations are recorded in [poland-to-netherlands-pricing.md](./poland-to-netherlands-pricing.md). The other categories remain unpriced until directly sourced product baskets and comparable labour inputs are established. Do not infer labour/material splits or apply a guide price to a different scope.
